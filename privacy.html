KS Analytics – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

KS Analytics is a private internal reporting tool operated by Koro Seraphim.

1. Data we collect

When Koro Seraphim’s own TikTok account is connected, KS Analytics collects only the data TikTok provides for the approved permissions:

  • Basic profile information (display name, avatar, and profile link)
  • Account statistics (follower count, following count, total likes, and video count)
  • The account’s public videos and their statistics (title, publish date, link, views, likes, comments, and shares)

KS Analytics does not collect data about other TikTok users, viewers, or followers. It does not collect passwords, messages, or payment information.

2. How we use it

The data is used only for internal performance reports to plan content. It is never sold, rented, or used for advertising.

3. Where it is stored

Data is stored in a private Google Sheet owned by Koro Seraphim and visible only to authorized team members. TikTok access tokens are stored privately in the sheet’s script settings. They are never displayed or shared.

4. Sharing

KS Analytics does not share this data with third parties, except where required by law.

5. Retention and deletion

Data is kept while it is useful for internal reporting. Access may be revoked at any time in the TikTok app (Settings and privacy > Security > Manage app permissions). On request, Koro Seraphim deletes stored TikTok data and tokens.

6. Changes

This privacy policy may be updated. The date at the top of this page shows the latest version.

7. Contact

Contact Koro Seraphim through the official Facebook page: https://www.facebook.com/koroseraphim.
