KS Analytics – Terms of Service

Last updated: November 17, 2026

1. About KS Analytics

KS Analytics is a private internal reporting tool operated by Koro Seraphim. It reads Koro Seraphim’s own social media statistics (follower counts, video views, likes, comments, and shares) and records them in a private Google Sheet used by the choir’s social media team.

2. Who may use it

Only Koro Seraphim’s authorized team members may use KS Analytics. It connects only to TikTok accounts owned or managed by Koro Seraphim. It is not offered to the public.

3. Connecting a TikTok account

By signing in with TikTok and approving access, the account owner allows KS Analytics to read basic profile information, account statistics, and the video list for the granted permissions. KS Analytics does not post, comment, message, follow, or change anything on the account.

4. Acceptable use

KS Analytics is for internal content planning and reporting only. Users must follow TikTok’s Terms of Service and Developer Terms.

5. Revoking access

Access may be revoked at any time from the TikTok app (Settings and privacy > Security > Manage app permissions) or by asking Koro Seraphim. After access is revoked, no new data is collected.

6. No warranty

KS Analytics is provided as is. Statistics come from TikTok and may be delayed or differ from what appears in the TikTok app.
